# PRIVACY POLICY
**PantryWise AI – Smart Kitchen & Food Manager**

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

PantryWise AI ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PantryWise AI.

Our goal is to provide a privacy-focused kitchen management experience with offline functionality and on-device AI processing wherever supported.

## 1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

Depending on the features you use, the App may store the following information:

**Pantry and Food Inventory**
- Food item names and categories.
- Ingredient quantities and measurement units.
- Purchase and expiry dates.
- Storage locations.
- Product names, brands and barcodes.
- Grocery prices and shopping history.

**User Preferences**
- Dietary preferences.
- Preferred cuisines.
- Serving sizes and household preferences.
- Favorite recipes and meal plans.
- Notification preferences.

**Photos and Camera Information**
- Food photographs captured using the camera.
- Product label images.
- Barcode scanning information.
- Text extracted from food packaging.

**AI Assistant Information**
- Questions entered into the AI cooking assistant.
- Saved conversations and generated recipe suggestions.
- Locally processed food recognition results.

This information is used to provide the App's features and is stored locally on your device wherever supported.

## 2. ON-DEVICE PROCESSING AND OFFLINE AI

PantryWise AI is designed to operate offline wherever possible.

Depending on your device and installed AI models:
- Food recognition may be performed using on-device machine learning.
- Product labels and expiry dates may be processed using Apple's Vision framework.
- Recipe recommendations may use locally stored recipe and ingredient databases.
- AI cooking assistance may use compatible on-device language models.

We do not send your pantry information, food photographs or AI conversations to external AI providers for processing as part of the offline AI functionality.

Some optional services or features may require an internet connection.

## 3. CAMERA AND PHOTO ACCESS

The App may request camera or photo library access to allow you to:
- Scan food items.
- Recognize ingredients.
- Scan product barcodes.
- Read food labels.
- Add product photographs to your inventory.

Camera and photo access are used only for the relevant functionality.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

## 4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

PantryWise AI is designed to store personal kitchen information locally on your device.

We do not require an account to use the basic pantry management features.

Your information may remain stored locally until you delete it or remove the App.

You are responsible for maintaining an appropriate backup of information you wish to preserve.

We use reasonable measures to protect locally stored information, but no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. NOTIFICATIONS

With your permission, the App may send local notifications relating to:

- Food expiry reminders.
- Low-stock ingredients.
- Meal planning.
- Grocery shopping.
- Other kitchen management reminders.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

## 8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Your locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it.

You can:
- Delete individual pantry items.
- Remove shopping lists.
- Delete saved recipes and conversations.
- Clear application data using available settings.
- Remove the App from your device.

Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to Apple's device backup and operating system behavior.

## 9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account registration system.

If you believe personal information has been collected from a child in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, please contact us.
